# Copy and sequences (paraphrased from three viewpoints; test, don't treat as law)

## Triple tap: three small wins per email
1. **Open**: sender name + subject + first line must look like normal business mail, not an ad. A question subject can work if the body matches it (no bait-and-switch).
2. **Read**: short, plain words (about sixth-grade level), up to roughly six sentences.
3. **Act**: a tiny reply ("want me to send it?"). Test: can they answer in a few words, one-handed?

## Email 1 structure (about 3 parts)
- **Who/why you**: either a *specific, checkable* observation about them (hiring, funding, launch, trial) or a plain one-line who-you-are. Never fake personalisation ("loved your recent video") when it isn't true.
- **Why relevant**: their persona's priority/pain and a result you delivered for similar firms, with the mechanism. One or two credibility facts (client, press, audience, a free pilot you did). No feature list, no jargon.
- **Ask**: pick one and match the person's seniority:
  - conversation starter: "Is this on your radar this year, or am I off base?"
  - soft meeting: "Worth 15 minutes to see if there's a fit?"
  - direct, assumptive: "I'd like to set up 15 minutes this week or next. What does your availability look like?"
  - lead-magnet: "Okay if I send over a short [guide/video/audit] for your business?"
Avoid passive "is this worth a chat?" wording. Avoid too-soft free-audit asks that attract freebie seekers, and be clear it's a sales conversation.

## Sequence (the sources disagree: test both)
- **High-volume template route**: email + 3 follow-ups, each 1-2 days apart (about 4 touches in a week).
- **Slower route**: 3 emails, 2-5 days apart; only extend to 4-7 for small markets when each email adds something new (proof, a different pain lever, an asset).
- Follow-up 1: "Making sure this reached you. Do either of these times work?" Follow-up 2: a short "What are your thoughts on this?" Follow-up 3: breakup: "Would next month be better? I'd like to close the loop either way."
- Follow-ups sit in the same thread and don't re-pitch. After the last touch, rest the contact; recycle only after 3-6 months with a new angle.
- Phone: for priority accounts, email one day, call the next.

## Plain-text hygiene (for deliverability)
No images, tracking pixels or HTML in email 1; no links in email 1 (later: only plain well-known links like a calendar or YouTube); keep copy free of spammy words; use light **spintax** variants so thousands of emails aren't identical; preview every variant. AI may write one short research snippet (2-8 words), checked by a human over ~100 samples; do not let AI write whole emails or whole lists.

## Warm outreach script (to people who know you)
"Hey [name], hope you're good! Quick one: do you ever deal with [problem]? I'm starting [service] and want to help 5-10 people for free/discounted to get it right. Anyone you'd introduce me to?" Track each contact and result.
