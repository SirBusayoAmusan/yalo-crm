# Templates (fill in the brackets)

## 1. Value newsletter
Subject: [Series name] #[n]: [one-line promise or stat]
Preview: [40-90 character curiosity line]
[Opener: quote, top post or stat]
Problem: [one sentence on what stops [audience] from [result]]
Fix: [one sentence naming the solution]
Tactic: [2-3 sentences, how to do it today, one example or number]
Sign-off, [name]
CTA: If you're [type A], [link] helps with [benefit]. If you're [type B], [link] helps with [benefit].
P.S. [bonus tip, meme or second reason to click]
Footer: [business name, address, unsubscribe link]
Rules: under 200 words, plain text, 1-2 links.

## 2. Welcome email
Subject: Your [freebie] is inside
Thanks for joining. Reply "yes" and I'll send [freebie] right away (it keeps our emails in your main inbox).
As a subscriber you also get [recurring benefit] and [subscriber-only extra].
Coming up in [timeframe]: [teaser]. Subscribers hear first.
Inside the freebie: [3-5 bullets].

## 3. Challenge invitation
Subject: [N]-day [outcome] challenge, starts [date]
Preview: Free. By day [N] you'll have [result].
[Who it's for.] Over [N] days you'll [specific outcome] using [simple method]. Each day takes about [time]. [Prize/leaderboard if any.]
Proof: [one real result or testimonial built on a painful moment]
Reserve your spot: [link]. Starts [date].
P.S. [deadline or finishing bonus]

## 4. Challenge sequence
Day 0 invite -> Day 1-2 reminder to non-registrants (new subject, same offer) -> daily lesson emails with one small win -> final lesson + paid offer with a real deadline -> day after: objections email -> testimonial email -> last call -> ascension offer to buyers.

## 5. Quarterly planner
Quarter / wrapper name / core deliverable (same underlying fix) / test segment (10-15%) / result to beat (revenue per email) / one variable to test / gate to expand (revenue per email at least $X, unsubscribes below Y%).

## 6. Contextual CTA bridge
"If [problem in this email] is costing you [consequence], [next step] shows you [benefit]: [link]." Beginners: "Just starting? [link] walks you through the first [step]."

## 7. Testimonial request that gives a hook
"Think back to the worst moment before we worked together. What was happening, and what number or detail made it hurt?" Then ask for the turn, the result with a figure, and the timeframe.

## 8. Annual renewal fee maths (recurring offers)
Monthly price P; renewal fee 1-3 times P charged after a year (state it in your terms). Year-one revenue 12P; year-two 12P + fee; lift = fee / 12P.
