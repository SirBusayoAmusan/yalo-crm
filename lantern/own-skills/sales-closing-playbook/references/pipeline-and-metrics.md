# Pipeline, speed and metrics (paraphrased)

## Opportunity multipliers (before the call)
- **Speed to lead**: contact new leads within about a minute where possible; response speed often explains "bad leads". Quoted source stats are claims, so treat as directional.
- **Availability**: evenings, weekends, same/next-day slots; same-day calls show more. Offer two specific times, not "when are you free?".
- **Pull appointments forward** into gaps today; move unconfirmed far-out calls back.
- **Two checklists**: an on-call script and an off-call routine (calling new leads, confirmations, follow-ups, pulling calls forward).
- **Show-up work**: reminders at 24h, morning-of, 1h before; ask a tiny personal preference question; personal voice or video note beats robotic texts. Don't pretend automation is human.
- **Lead routing**: best leads to best closers, new reps get lower-value leads; don't round-robin by default.
- **Referrals**: end every call with "Who do you know who's as great as you and would benefit?" (needs a name, not yes/no). Roughly 1 in 3-4 refer; referrals close far better.
- **Kill list**: a visible daily list of best prospects.

## Metrics (track all, fix the weakest first)
lead -> booked -> show -> offer made -> close -> cash collected, plus calls per close, follow-up counts, response time. A 20% gain in show rate is worth about as much as 20% on close rate. Track offer rate too, so a high close rate isn't just cherry-picking easy leads.

## Training habits
- Daily short role-plays, one focus at a time; instant feedback; repeat the corrected line several times.
- Script adherence first, then change the script if it does not close. Record calls and review weekly (sales plus customer success).
- Study your best ("hot") calls for tone, opener, transition to the close; systemise them.
- Slumps: re-read real testimonials; conviction improves tone more than drilling.
- Managers: the best closer isn't automatically the best coach.
- Never blame leads, comp or circumstances; give feedback as data, privately.

## Diagnose
| Symptom | Likely fix |
|---|---|
| Few calls booked | speed to lead, availability, outreach volume |
| Booked but no shows | same-day booking, reminders, edify the closer |
| Shows but no offers | weak discovery/qualifying, agenda, rambling |
| Offers but no closes | obstacles after price, no ask-again loop, weak pain |
| "Think about it" deaths | surface obstacles pre-price, stall questions, BAMFAM |
